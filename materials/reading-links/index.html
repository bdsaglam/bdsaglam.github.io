1. Science, Math & Physics

The Feynman Lectures on PhysicsS

Mechanics, electromagnetism, quantum mechanics, and physical intuition from first principles.

Richard Feynman (Nobel laureate in physics); Caltech's free online edition of his canonical undergraduate lectures.

BetterExplainedS

Calculus, linear algebra, imaginary numbers, and probability through visual and intuitive analogies.

Kalid Azad; focuses on building mental models of math concepts before formal symbol manipulation.

3Blue1BrownS

Linear algebra, calculus, differential equations, neural networks, and information theory.

Grant Sanderson; creator of the Manim animation engine, known for visual geometric proofs.

Quanta MagazineS

Research breakthroughs in mathematics, theoretical physics, theoretical computer science, and biology.

Editorially independent science publication funded by the Simons Foundation; rigorous yet accessible reporting on active research.

What's newS

Analysis, combinatorics, PDEs, random matrices, and mathematical exposition.

Terence Tao (UCLA professor, Fields Medalist); mixes research updates, expository notes, and career advice for mathematicians.

The Theoretical MinimumS

Classical mechanics, quantum mechanics, special relativity, and statistical mechanics.

Leonard Susskind (Stanford physicist, co-founder of string theory); bridges popular science and graduate-level physics.

Simons Institute — Workshops & SymposiaS

Theoretical computer science, machine learning theory, complexity, and optimization.

UC Berkeley's premier theory institute; hosts recorded semester-long workshops and talks by leading researchers.

NautilusA

Cross-disciplinary essays connecting physics, neuroscience, biology, and philosophy.

Long-form science magazine organized around monthly thematic issues.

Landmarks in Physics (YouTube)A

Introductory physics structured around historical experiments and breakthroughs.

Udacity course playlist teaching classical physics through landmark discoveries.

Scientific AmericanB

General science news, health, climate, physics, and technology.

Oldest continuously published US monthly science magazine; broad coverage for general readers.
