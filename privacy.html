Privacy and cookies

This is a personal website. It has no accounts, comments, or ads, and I don’t sell or share data with anyone.

Analytics, only if you accept

If you accept cookies in the banner, the site loads Google Analytics 4 to count visits and see which pages people read. It sets two cookies, _ga and _ga_498T1C2SN2, which last up to two years. Google Analytics 4 does not log or store IP addresses (Google). Google processes this data under its privacy policy.

If you decline or ignore the banner, Google Analytics never loads.

Your choice

The banner remembers your choice in one cookie, analytics_consent, for six months. You can change it at any time through cookie preferences at the bottom of every page; declining also deletes the Google Analytics cookies.

Other services

Fonts are served from this site itself. A few other requests set no cookies, but they send your IP address to the service:

  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log visitor IP addresses for security (GitHub’s privacy statement).
  • Pages with equations load MathJax from the jsDelivr CDN, and a small compatibility script from Cloudflare’s cdnjs.
  • Course notebooks show an “Open in Colab” badge loaded from Google Colab.
  • The slide decks under /slides/ load their fonts from Google Fonts.
